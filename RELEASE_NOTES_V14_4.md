# GeoStats v14.4.0

## Rebuild goals

v14.4 replaces the overlapping v14.2–v14.3.1 eligibility gates with one transparent computed playability policy and strengthens Daily generation as a three-board package.

## Included

- Computed playability with explicit blockers and warnings
- Exact or safe general official player source pages
- No API, raw-file, bulk-download, or attachment links exposed to players
- Non-destructive player-link auditing
- Cross-mode semantic-family diversity
- Hard global top-30 board-winner validation
- At most one shared country between any two Daily modes
- Atomic three-board persistence and post-save validation
- Preservation of scored boards
- Detailed generation diagnostics
- Exact raw category-combination counts plus estimated rule-valid capacity for all three modes
- Next.js 16 `proxy.ts` convention
- Visible `GITHUB_ACTIONS/workflows` copy alongside the required hidden folder
- Consolidated install, verification, rollback, and repository checks
