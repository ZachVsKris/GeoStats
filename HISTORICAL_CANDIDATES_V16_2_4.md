# Historical category curation after v16.2.4

## Product rule

Historical categories should be broad, intuitive, distinct, and reasonably guessable by a normal geography/history player. Prefer one strong category for a concept over multiple subtle variations. A source being available is not enough if the question is specialist trivia.

Do **not** split concepts into near-duplicates such as earliest Homo sapiens remains vs. earliest hominin remains, or earliest city vs. earliest fortified city vs. earliest planned city. Do not add World Heritage milestone dates, Ramsar, biosphere-reserve milestones, postal-union membership, or similarly niche institutional milestones.

## Shipping in v16.2.4

The four v16.2.3 historical categories remain:

- Most recently admitted to the UN
- Oldest current constitution
- Most recently became independent
- Earliest universal women's suffrage

v16.2.4 adds four reproducible World Bank milestones:

- Most recently became majority urban
- Most recently reached 50% internet use
- Most recently reached 50% electricity access
- Most recently reached 70-year life expectancy

## Highest-priority future candidates needing a consistent authoritative global dataset

These remain strong concepts but are **not** silently shipped without source coverage adequate for the GeoStats ranking gate:

### Ancient and long-run history
- Oldest continuously inhabited city within present-day borders
- Earliest known writing within present-day borders
- Earliest well-supported agriculture milestone within present-day borders
- Earliest well-supported coinage milestone within present-day borders

Ancient dates must support uncertainty/approximation explicitly; countries should not be compared when plausible archaeological date ranges overlap enough to change the ordering.

### State and national identity
- First national constitution
- Most recent current constitution
- Current national flag adoption / oldest current national flag
- Current national anthem adoption / oldest current anthem
- Current currency introduction / oldest current currency
- Current capital became capital / most recently changed capital
- Most recently became a republic
- Complete legal abolition of slavery
- First national census

- Universal male suffrage

## Curation discipline

A candidate should ship only when:

1. the definition is clear enough to explain in one short sentence;
2. it represents a genuinely different historical idea from categories already in play;
3. a normal player can make an educated guess using broad knowledge;
4. the source supports enough current countries for a meaningful ranking;
5. chronology and ties can be represented without false precision; and
6. the category passes the same source, semantic, ranking, and playable-catalog gates as every other Daily/Random category.
