# Additional mammal emphasis

Added nine distinct concepts (18 paired proposed prize labels): tongue length, licking frequency, social grooming time, male/female mass ratio, age at first birth, urine concentration, urination duration, sound localization, and feeding suction. Total review: 162 concepts / 322 proposed prize labels. Zero new playable approvals.

User preference: mammals may have greater weight because they are familiar; continue expansion in insects, fish and other invertebrates too. Equal numbers of research ideas per clade are not required. Board variety and at least half intuitive comparisons remain required.

Seven additions are marked intuitive, two specialist. Urination is held because interspecies differences may not support reliable rankings. All others require source-level audit before gameplay. Papers located are evidence leads, not acquired measurement tables.

Strong additional mammal source: Dryad 10.5061/dryad.280gb5mx0 provides sex-specific adult mass means, sample sizes, uncertainties and source references; README reports 666 raw rows, while the paper reports 429 species after filtering. These are different counts. Zenodo 10569230 holds code only. No missing values, immature animals, model predictions or cross-population ratios may be silently substituted. Ratios from measured means are transparent arithmetic and require explicit editorial approval.

Tongue anatomical length and operational extension are distinct. Grooming means observed allogrooming time, not fitted predictions. Urine concentration is a specialist endpoint and does not imply drinking independence. Fish suction pressure is not model-estimated suction performance.

Primary links are included beside every paired proposal in discovery-category-register.csv and the searchable review. Source coverage enhancements and old memory-test leads were not counted as new concepts.
