AnimalStats category review

162 measurement/attribute concepts, proposing 322 prize categories. Opposites stay together and each counts separately; one-sided categories are allowed. “Keep” means worth pursuing; it does not mean ready to play. No new game categories are approved by this review.

Balance gap: current playable pool has 35 mammals, 31 birds, 6 reptiles and 5 fish (sharks). Insects and spiders are absent. Large research catalogs do not establish balanced playable boards. Geographic balance is still unverified for most endpoints.
