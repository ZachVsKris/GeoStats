# AnimalStats quality release, October 9, 2026

## Changes

- Cute / Static labels; preserve existing presentation preferences and choices.
- Six distinct podium ribbon colors, including tails.
- Results ordered by points earned, using shared GeoStats results components.
- Source and catalog requests time out after 12 seconds; saved ranking CSV remains available if the source request fails.
- Manual challenge-copy dialog contains the actual challenge URL.
- Daily has one explicitly reviewed board per difficulty; New random board leaves Daily.
- Withheld challenge URLs explain the updated checks and show an eligible replacement.
- Animation controls appear only in Cute mode.
- Full data catalog includes approved photo records.

## Publication policy

Count unique active, familiar catalog animals with approved, non-imputed observations matching the pinned source, unit, measurement basis and eligible groups. Broad comparisons require 50 animals and at least three major groups. Explicitly scoped comparisons require 20 animals; breed comparisons require eight breeds. Opposite directions are published together. These are editorial floors, not claims of scientific global completeness. Source rank scope is shown in definitions and data views.

The release audit retains 2,192 default boards, 66 distinct display labels, 83 source-specific directions and 117 played animals. All 4,040 candidate boards pass structural validation; 1,782 fail the new coverage gate. Simulated rotation reaches every eligible label and animal in each difficulty over 3,000 draws without immediately repeating a lineup. Existing intuitive-majority and common-animal composition requirements remain active.

## Limits

The 200+ label expansion and full GeoStats parity remain unfinished. This is not a fresh verification of every numerical observation against its original publication. The initial reviewed Daily set has only one board per difficulty and repeats until more boards are reviewed. Photo scenery still varies inside the uniform neutral frames. Local UI checks do not establish live account writes, email delivery or report submission. Broader taxonomy coverage, including insects, needs additional trustworthy data and complete boards.
