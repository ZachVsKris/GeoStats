# Broader AnimalStats discovery pass

The combined review proposes 304 prize categories across 153 distinct measurements/attributes. Each opposite counts separately and remains adjacent on the list. Two one-sided concepts are included. This pass adds 41 concepts / 80 proposed prizes, with 36 initially intuitive and five specialist. These counts include existing categories, evidence leads and unresolved ideas; they are not newly playable content.

Editorial recommendations now cover 103 keep, 45 hold and five reject concepts. The underlying 112-concept inventory is preserved; the additional register is joined when building the review. Keep means pursue, not approve for the game. New game approvals remain zero.

## Acquisitions and verified distinctions

- Mammal heart/breathing research: author repository files pinned and verified against Git blob hashes. A strict adult/resting/unsedated filter yields 32 heart-rate rows (31 source binomials) and 76 breathing-rate rows (76 source binomials). Awake status, primary references and measurement context remain checks. Predictions are excluded.
- Northwest European arthropods: acquired Darwin Core archive with 4,874 taxon entries and 30,603 trait rows, including 438 spiders, 34 woodlice, 29 harvestmen and seven insect orders. Field counts include 581 development-time, 425 fecundity and 192 lifespan entries. Expert-derived values and relative dispersal scores remain held. Thermal niche does not measure physiological tolerance. Source license is CC-BY-NC-4.0.
- Fish reproduction: actual XLSX payload acquired from PANGAEA, despite a text-style request URL. Some rows are genus-level or inferred; these must be excluded. Egg counts/diameters overlap existing concepts and were not counted as new concepts.
- Call repertoire: acquired bird and primate source tables. Differences in call definitions and research effort prevent immediate pooling.
- Whisker and grip measurements: source workbooks acquired. Whisker identity and length-versus-span, plus specimen-to-species keys and grip test protocols, need audit.
- Field energy: spreadsheet acquired; not yet parsed or approved.
- Mammal prey size: original supplementary PDF acquired and text extraction checked. No automatic numeric import.

## Leads and rejected interpretations

Milk composition, chewing speed, food retention, skin and shell properties, eye field, eye count, limbs, reflexes, claws and silk properties have primary-source leads. They are not all acquired numerical datasets. Milk supplement download returned HTML, not ZIP; it is recorded as a failed payload. The current spider venom repository offers code/phylogeny files, not the main S1 table. The fish-oxygen source is verified at Cefas but no observations were downloaded; an initially guessed PMC accession resolved to an unrelated lizard paper and was excluded.

The Animal Culture Database tables are acquired, but a documented tool-user attribute is not a generic intelligence score. Missing cultural observations do not mean an animal cannot use tools. This one-sided concept requires a separate gameplay decision.
