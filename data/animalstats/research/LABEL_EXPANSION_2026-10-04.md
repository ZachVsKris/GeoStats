# Label expansion: 4 October 2026

84 distinct playable labels, up from 78; 94 category IDs, 2,740 candidate rounds.

New adjacent pairs:
- Longest intestine / Shortest intestine
- Heaviest at weaning / Lightest at weaning
- Latest male maturity / Earliest male maturity

Intestine lengths come from the 2021 Duque-Correa et al. original adult-anatomy compilation. Exact species only, at least three animals (eight eligible species), no modeled lengths, relative-diet substitutions or maxima inferred from averages. Source: https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.z8w9ghxb8 . Archive CC0 license confirmed in its metadata. Mixed wild/captive cohorts and missing uncertainty are disclosed in each record.

Weaning mass and male maturity activate existing exact-species AnAge measurements (19 and 94 species). Male and female maturity share a metric family; weaning mass and weaning age share a life-stage family. Scout and Adventurer retain at most one life-history prize, with at least half intuitive categories on every board. Opposite directions have the same observations and never appear together.

All 4,989 earlier observations are unchanged. Retained shared boards keep their identifiers, animal order and trait order. Daily approval remains bound to reviewed data; these additions do not silently grant daily approval.

Further research remains necessary for visual processing, hearing, thermal, salt and oxygen tolerance. The 237-species visual compilation contains domestic-dog observations mapped to Canis lupus and inconsistent chicken method labels, making a blind import unsuitable. No wolf vision inference is approved.

Validation: production webpack build passed; source/board invariants and previously shared board retention passed. Browser checks cover scoring, full data responses, phone layout and both-direction placement.
