## Bulk ecology source

TetrapodTraits 2.0.1 contains 33,281 species. The release admits mapped range, mean habitat temperature, rainfall, elevation, food-group diversity, non-imputed habitat diversity and assessed IUCN category for up to 180 selected species. Opposites share each observation. No imputed body mass, length, threat status or developmental mode is used. Mixed litter statistics and longevity without wild/captive context remain excluded. Original sources, snapshot versions and upstream units are retained. Bird maps represent breeding range; habitat climate is not animal tolerance. Kelvin storage is displayed in Celsius without changing source comparisons.

Source: https://zenodo.org/records/18926700 (CC BY 4.0). Cite Moura et al. 2024, doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.3002658, and Pyron et al. 2025, doi:10.21203/rs.3.rs-7556378/v1.

## Gameplay and media

8,736 eligible boards: 4,232 mammals, 1,404 reptiles, 1,400 amphibians, 38 fish, 147 birds, 293 dog breeds, 2 cat breeds, 10 mixed and 1,210 extinct dinosaurs. 134 distinct display labels. Previous published group-board IDs are retained. New rounds have at least three intuitive prizes out of four, independently winning animals, matching sex/life-stage definitions, coverage floors and rank separation. At most two habitat-climate prizes occur together. Generation avoids always selecting the first animals and caps new variants per lineup. Bats are limited to one animal in generated mammal rounds.

Five new mammals have reviewed licensed photographs and species-specific cartoon designs: African buffalo, impala, aardvark, gray squirrel and North American river otter. Seven further fish photographs are reviewed and ready; their additional round eligibility still depends on enough compatible facts. Other added animals may appear in rankings before they have approved media for rounds.

Every generated board passed validation, composition and perfect-score checks. TypeScript and production build passed. Insect and other invertebrate-only rounds remain zero; this release does not claim completion of those groups or the 200-category target.
