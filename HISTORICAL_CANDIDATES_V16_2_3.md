# Broad historical candidates after v16.2.3

Product filter: a normal geography/history fan should be able to make an educated guess from broad country knowledge. A good data source is not enough if the milestone itself is niche.

## Strong candidates needing a consistent authoritative global dataset

- First men's FIFA World Cup appearance
- First modern Olympic appearance
- First national satellite launched
- Year the current national flag was adopted / oldest current flag
- Most recently changed national capital
- Most recently became a republic
- Earliest or most recent complete legal abolition of slavery
- Oldest current currency, with a strict continuity definition
- Oldest current national anthem, lower priority

## Explicitly out of scope

Do not add World Heritage inscription dates, biosphere reserves, Ramsar wetlands, postal-union membership, first woman in parliament/speaker, or similarly specific institutional milestones. Universal women's suffrage is the one approved women-specific historical concept.
