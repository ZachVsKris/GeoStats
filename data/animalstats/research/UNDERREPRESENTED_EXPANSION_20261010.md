# Underrepresented group expansion

Released: 40 reptile-only boards (36 Scout, 4 Adventurer), 19 distinct lineups. Every board passes the same published coverage, intuitive majority, familiarity, separation, distinct-winner and perfect-score checks. Existing source observations are reused; no new biological values approved.

The live catalog now has 3,938 rotation-eligible boards. The 120 distinct display labels are unchanged. Fish, amphibian and insect/invertebrate themed board counts remain zero; this is an unfinished expansion, not a balanced release.

A reproducible board-mix audit now records every group, including zero-count groups. Rotation recognizes fish, insects and other invertebrates separately so they can receive independent exposure once qualified boards exist. Mammal coverage classification now includes bats, monotremes, insectivores, lagomorphs and treeshrews instead of treating them as separate major classes.

Bulk field review covers ReptTraits, AmphiBIO, FishBase, SeaLifeBase and a newly acquired northwestern European arthropod source. Nine tables contain 182,547 raw rows and 32 reviewed candidate fields, with duplicates and repeated species. These counts are not unique animals, new categories or vetted game values.

Useful candidate fields include adult size/weight, lifespan, offspring counts, hatching/newborn size, development time, breeding frequency and actual movement distance. Modeled weights, mixed length types, ambiguous egg/offspring definitions, seasonal fecundity, midpoint summaries, expert estimates and unresolved licenses remain held. Trait opposites remain adjacent in review, never required together on a board.
