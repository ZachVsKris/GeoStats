# GeoStats v15.6.2

## Policy correction

GeoStats now has one approved gameplay catalog. A category is either approved
for Daily, Random, and Seeded, or it is quarantined, duplicate, retired, or
blocked by a hard data-integrity requirement.

## Daily stability

- Physical-geography quantity is a preference rather than a fatal validity gate
- Added balanced, recovery, and availability-first generation profiles
- Retained tie-free displayed values, global top-30 winners, distinct winners,
  semantic conflict checks, dimensions, and country-overlap limits
- Added trio-wide emissions and service-composition family caps
- Added category-set-version validation and safe regeneration of unscored boards
- Simplified player-facing generation errors while preserving internal diagnostics

## Copy corrections

- Most CO₂ emissions per person
- Highest communications and IT export share
- Highest communications and IT import share
- Highest transport share of service exports
- Highest transport share of service imports
- Highest travel share of service exports
- Highest travel share of service imports

A catalog-wide copy-audit table now identifies the remaining approved titles and
descriptions that need the planned manual review.
