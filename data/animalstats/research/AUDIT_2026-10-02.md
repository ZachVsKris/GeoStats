# AnimalStats source and category audit — October 2, 2026

This is a field-level editorial screen and a targeted insect readiness review. It is not a claim that every imported observation has been validated. No new bulk-imported record has been automatically approved for gameplay.

## Current imbalance

The local candidate pool contains 35 mammal species, 31 birds, six reptiles and five fish (all sharks). It has no playable insects or spiders. Across candidate slots, mammals account for 79.2%, birds 7.8%, fish 7.6%, and reptiles 5.4%. These are candidate-pool counts, not actual player sessions or runtime sampling probabilities. Mammals need greater fact variety; insects and non-shark fish need admission coverage. Large reptile tables should not drive rotation.

## Field decisions

651 populated source fields were screened across 17 research catalogs. The screen retains 97 source fields as candidates, consolidating them into 24 concepts. This does not create 24 new game categories: several concepts already exist. 429 fields remain context, 49 specialist fields are deferred, 71 fields are unsuitable for precise numeric prizes, and five are held for comparability review. See `screened-traits.csv` for field-level reasons.

Prioritize familiar concepts: mass, length, lifespan, wingspan, bite force, offspring count, pregnancy, incubation, range, social-group size and diet variety. Precise endpoint review comes before a short front-facing label. Shoulder height is excluded from the playable expansion, and is not renamed overall height. Do not invent strength, total worldwide abundance, lifetime distance, or overall height from proxies in these tables.

Specialist supplements include brain mass, metabolism, newborn mass, egg dimensions, tail/beak measurements, milk duration, habitat depth and larval hostplant breadth. Several are interesting individually, but they cannot be used to fill a board with closely related reproductive or anatomical decisions.

## Source-specific findings

| Source | Useful direction | Required restriction |
|---|---|---|
| LepTraits | Butterfly wingspan | Consensus endpoint averages are not measured world maxima. Preserve sex, source units and source-record ranges. Duplicate names must not silently overwrite conflicting rows. |
| Odonate database | Dragonfly/damselfly body length | Multiple source rows, sex-specific fields and text-encoded observations require explicit parsing and reconciliation. Wing length is not wingspan. |
| Insect bite dataset | Bite force and body length | Count independent specimens, not bite series; keep specimen variability and assay position. Zero wing fields are ambiguous. Do not compare directly with modeled mammal bite forces. |
| FishBase / SeaLifeBase | Size and longevity beyond sharks | Preserve total/standard/fork length, sex and wild/captive basis; join primary reference IDs. Modeled vulnerability is excluded. Depth is habitat occurrence, not diving performance. |
| COMBINE reported | Mammal behavior and broader basic facts | Use reported table, not imputed table. “Reported” does not ensure every upstream figure is directly measured; inspect per-trait source references. |
| PanTHERIA | Mammal group size, diet and range | Its dictionary allows mixed methods and primary/secondary/extrapolated sources in several biological fields, even without an EXT suffix. Diet breadth counts broad categories. Aggregated group values need source-level checks. Historical map areas are dated mapped extent. |
| CarniDIET | Evidence of mammal diets | Frequency, biomass, volume and kills differ. Recorded prey richness depends on study effort; it cannot become an unqualified “most diverse diet” ranking. |
| DISPERSE / BIOTIC | Discovery and ecological context | Ordinal/fuzzy-coded bands are not exact measurements; genus/family records cannot be assigned to a species. |
| GlobTherm | Potential temperature-tolerance comparisons | Separate CTmax, lethal thresholds and thermoneutral bounds, acclimation and exposure protocols. No pooled generic temperature rank. |
| SquamBase | Reptile length and some life history | Allometric masses excluded. Measurement methods and sexes stay explicit. No rotation quota based on the table's size. |

## Targeted insect review

Fourteen recognizable insect species were checked against the bite-force catalog and other source catalogs. Five meet the minimum three-independent-specimen screen: Western honey bee, two-spotted cricket, emperor dragonfly, banded demoiselle and black-tailed skimmer. This screen alone is not gameplay approval. The migratory locust has one specimen in this assay and is held out under that rule.

Twenty insect accounts were requested from Animal Diversity Web; eight yielded usable account sections. Missing accounts are not evidence of missing traits in nature. Account-level traps include queen versus worker lifespan, larval versus adult lifespan, egg development called “gestation,” seasonal egg counts versus brood counts, and butterfly size fields that may describe wingspan rather than body length.

Anatomy plus bite force is not enough to establish a varied insect board. The next observation reviews should prioritize life history and behavior from original studies, with recognizable species across regions. Avoid disguising head, body and wing dimensions as independent gameplay variety.

## Promotion requirements

For each selected observation: scientific identity, source reference, measurement method, units, sex/life stage, summary endpoint, missing-value handling and uncertainty must be explicit. Source labels stay precise in the data panel. Counter-prizes reverse the identical scalar records. No invented midpoint, inferred absence, estimated population, or modeled biological value enters the reviewed game dataset.

Before promotion, verify the intersection of reviewed facts for actual animal lineups. Every board must have at least half intuitive categories, preferably more, and distinct meaningful trait families and winners. Audit worldwide spread and actual appearances after generation. The existing release must not be advertised as balanced solely because new catalogs have been downloaded.
